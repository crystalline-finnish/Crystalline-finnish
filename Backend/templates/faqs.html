FAQs

Common questions

How long does installation take? +

Most residential installations take 1–3 days depending on the number of openings. Commercial shopfronts and large office partitions typically take 3–7 days.

Do you offer warranties? +

Yes — A 5-Year Warranty on Glass Discolouration and a 1-Year Warranty on Aluminium Profiles

Can you customize sizes? +

Yes, all windows and doors are fabricated to the exact dimensions of your opening — there is no fixed standard size requirement.

Do you install countrywide? +

Yes, we serve all 47 counties. Sites outside Nairobi may include a logistics fee, confirmed during your quote.

Do I need to pay for the site measurement? +

No, the initial site visit and measurement within Nairobi are free of charge and carry no obligation to proceed.
